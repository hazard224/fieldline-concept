What we do

What working with Salient looks like.

We’re not here to dictate decisions or replace key roles. We combine human expertise with advanced data technology to support distributed decision-making without losing alignment.

Align performance to purpose

We work with leaders to define what success looks like, where performance matters most and how responsibility should be distributed to drive outcomes.

Create an AI-ready data foundation

Salient integrates, organizes, normalizes and validates data across systems, teams and partners so performance can be measured consistently and trusted.

Make data meaningful to the business

We don’t stop at moving data from one place to another. We connect it to value, responsibility and action so teams understand what it means.

Enable decision-making at every level

We put decision-ready information into the hands of the people closest to the work while maintaining visibility and accountability across the enterprise.

Support investigation, not just reporting

We design for exploration, context and root cause so teams understand why performance changed and what to do next.

Connect performance intelligence to the AI ecosystem

Validated performance data, business context and interactive exploration become accessible to the people, systems and AI tools that need them.

Partner beyond implementation

We stay engaged as performance evolves, helping organizations adapt, expand and keep improving as priorities and conditions change.

Sustain accountability over time

Shared measures, transparent feedback loops and systems that reinforce ownership long after implementation.

Built to change with you

Questions

Questions leaders ask us

Do we have to fit our business into your model?

No. We start with the realities of your operating environment instead of asking you to translate them into a generic application or predetermined model. Measures, hierarchies, business rules and allocations are built around the way your organization creates value.

What kinds of data can Salient bring together?

It depends on your business, but commonly shipments, depletions, sales, inventory, pricing, promotions, financial costs, labor, delivery and cost-to-serve, retail scans and syndicated market data. Salient connects, cleans and validates it into one governed foundation.

Who uses Salient day to day?

The people responsible for outcomes: category and merchandising teams, store and route operations, sales, finance, marketing and leadership. Each sees what’s relevant to their role while working from a shared understanding of performance.

How does AI fit in?

Integrated AI helps users navigate, analyze, interpret change and find where to investigate next. Because it works inside an environment structured around your measures and business rules, it has the context to support relevant analysis. Salient also helps make validated performance data available to the broader AI tools your organization uses.

What happens after implementation?

We stay engaged. As priorities and conditions change, we incorporate new data, refine measures and business rules, extend visibility to new areas and introduce new capabilities.

Where do we start?

Whether you’ve defined goals or have early questions, start here. We’ll guide the path forward with you.
